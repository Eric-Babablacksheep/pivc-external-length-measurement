Capture guidance

Prepare a measurable image

  • Keep the complete hub and insertion-side endpoint in frame.
  • Show at least two consecutive graduation marks clearly.
  • Hold the camera approximately perpendicular to the surface.
  • Keep the PIVC near the centre without cropping either end.
  • Avoid motion blur, strong glare and deep shadows.
