Method and limitations

Research guide

Image acquisition

Keep both PIVC endpoints and two consecutive marks visible. Use a perpendicular camera angle and match follow-up framing to the baseline.

Measurement method

The model segments the PIVC and marks. Post-processing reconstructs a continuous centreline and converts pixels using the known 1 cm mark spacing.

Change indicators

Signed change is follow-up minus baseline. Values inside the session tolerance are reported as within measurement tolerance.

Rejected measurements

Unsafe segmentation, centreline or calibration results are rejected. A rejected follow-up never replaces the baseline.

Known limitations

Image angle, glare, dressing appearance, segmentation confidence and visible marks can affect reliability. Sessions are temporary.

Non-clinical status

This proof of concept is not clinically validated and must not guide patient care.
